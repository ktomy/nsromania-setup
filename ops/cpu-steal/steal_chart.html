CPU steal

Time our vCPUs were ready to run but the hypervisor gave the physical CPU to something else: capacity we pay for and do not get.

Our CPU use

Work our services got done (user + nice + system + IRQ time) as a share of all vCPUs. Our use + steal + idle = 100%.

Load average (1 min)

Tasks running or waiting for a CPU. Waiting caused by steal raises it too, so it can climb without any extra work.

Generated by ops/cpu-steal/chart.sh from the nsromania-cpu-steal collector, which reads /proc/stat every 5 seconds and writes one line per minute. Steal is the same counter as top/vmstat/sar "st". Effective vCPUs = vCPUs × (1 − steal). Idle includes iowait (about 0.1%).

Points are averages over the chosen resolution, drawn at the middle of each period. The tooltip also shows the highest 5-second steal sample in the period.
